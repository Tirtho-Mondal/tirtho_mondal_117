Preprint arXiv · April 2025

Real-Time Assistive Navigation for the Visually Impaired

Dabbrata Das, Argho Deb Das, Farhan Sadaf, Azhar Uddin, Tirtho Mondal

Co-author 5 authors arXiv:2504.20976
First Page
Abstract

Navigating unfamiliar environments remains one of the most persistent challenges for people who are blind or have low vision (BLV). Existing assistive tools often rely on online services or APIs, making them costly, internet-dependent, and less reliable in real time. We propose PathFinder, a novel mapless mobile-phone-based navigation system that operates fully offline. Our method processes monocular depth images and applies an efficient pathfinding algorithm to identify the longest, clearest obstacle-free route, ensuring optimal navigation with low computational cost. Comparative evaluations show that PathFinder reduces mean absolute error (MAE), speeds decision-making, and achieves real-time responsiveness indoors and outdoors. A usability study with 15 BLV participants confirmed its practicality: 73% learned to operate it in under a minute, and 80% praised its accuracy, responsiveness, and convenience.
